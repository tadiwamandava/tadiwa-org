## Storage and recovery

The server accepts Redis-compatible commands through RESP. An unmodified
`redis-cli` can connect and issue `GET`, `SET`, and `DEL`.

A write-ahead log records changes before they enter the memtable. Immutable
SSTables hold flushed data, with sparse indexes and Bloom filters to narrow
reads. Background compaction merges the files while clients continue to read
and write.

The fsync policy controls the durability/throughput tradeoff. Recovery tests
interrupt the server during writes and check the resulting state. Property-based
tests compare operation sequences with a reference HashMap, including sequences
that cross a compaction or recovery boundary.

## Why I built it

I could draw the storage engines behind the backends I used, but I could not
explain their recovery behaviour in much detail. Implementing one made me work
through the ordering of writes, file creation, and manifest updates. A crash
between any two of those steps still needs a recoverable state.
