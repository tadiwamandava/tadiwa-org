## Why this is on the site

Signal processing is the most direct bridge between the coursework and the
industry work I am aiming at. Fixed-point arithmetic, Q-formats, and the
question of where numerical error concentrates are the daily material of on-device
AI teams — the same discipline that shows up again in int8 and int4 quantisation
on the inference engine, and again in CMSIS-NN kernels on a microcontroller.

<Callout kind="note">
This is coursework rather than independent work, and it is labelled that way on
purpose. It earns its place by being the foundation the quantisation projects
build on, not by pretending to be more than it is.
</Callout>
