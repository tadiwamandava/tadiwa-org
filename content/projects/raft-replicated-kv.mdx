## Why distributed systems

It is where undergraduate portfolios are weakest, which means demonstrated
competence reads several years above your age. It is also the purest form of the
system-level thinking that hiring managers say they are scanning for.

## The arc

Leader election, then log replication, then persistence, then a linearizable
key-value service on top, each against a test harness explicitly designed to be
adversarial about network partitions, reordering, and crashes.

Then the part that makes it mine rather than a lab submission: put it in front of
the LSM engine, so writes commit through consensus and my storage engine is the
replicated state machine. The demo is killing the leader mid-write-storm on video
and showing re-election with zero committed-write loss, backed by a
linearizability checker rather than a claim.
